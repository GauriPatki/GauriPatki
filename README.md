I'm a **recent M.S. graduate in Data Science** from the **University of Wisconsin–Madison** (GPA: 3.65/4.0), with a strong foundation in machine learning, deep learning, NLP, and AI systems engineering.

My work spans **computer vision**, **retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)**, **predictive analytics**, and **stochastic optimization** — always focused on building systems that solve real problems with measurable impact.

### 🔬 AI-Assisted Urinalysis
**Feb 2025 – May 2025 · UW–Madison**

Benchmarked **YOLOv8**, **Detectron2 (Faster R-CNN)**, and a custom **CNN** to detect and classify 7 urinary sediment particle types from 15,185 microscopic images. Tackled extreme class imbalance with rotational augmentation via Roboflow.

| Model | Precision | Recall |
|-------|-----------|--------|
| YOLOv8-m | **0.85** | **0.86** |
| Detectron2 | 0.57 avg | 0.74 avg |
| CNN | 0.53 macro | 0.49 macro |

`YOLOv8` `Detectron2` `PyTorch` `Computer Vision` `Medical AI`

🔗 [View Repository](https://github.com/GauriPatki/AI-Assisted-Urinalysis-Robust-Detection-of-Microscopic-Sediments-via-Deep-Object-Detection-Models)

### 🏥 OR Scheduling via Stochastic Optimization
**Sep 2024 – Dec 2024 · UW–Madison**
